# Content Voice

Two registers. Pick one per piece — don't blend them.

## Quiet-personal (journal; most essays)
Small concrete opening, short plain sentences, first person, admits uncertainty rather than asserting conclusions, ends on a grounded image rather than a stated lesson. Journal is always this register — 300–500 words is too short for the one below.

## Literary-critical (optional — essays about a specific work, prize, translation, or critical debate)
Modeled on *Kritikus Sastera* (azriq.blogspot.com), studied 2026-08-17 — voice and structure only, never its opinions or text.
- Opens from a real anecdote or specific reference, not a generic hook.
- Builds via rhetorical questions that expose a tension, not flat assertions.
- Alternates short declaratives with long meditative sentences; can run a whole section as one unbroken paragraph.
- One controlling metaphor, returned to rather than decorated with.
- Closes on a resonant image, not a stated conclusion.
- Minimal humor; elegiac when the subject is loss, obscurity, or asymmetry.

## BM adaptations
Adapt the rhythm naturally into Malay — never a literal translation, in either direction.
