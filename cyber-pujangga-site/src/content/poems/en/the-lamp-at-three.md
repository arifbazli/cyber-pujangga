The lamp at three in the morning
is not the same lamp as the one at noon.

It does not flatter.
It does not pretend the desk is something other than a desk.

It is the lamp of those who have stayed
past the point where staying has anything to prove.

I write, and the page receives me
without judgment, without the small lies of daylight.
